Relay

An unofficial desktop client for WhatsApp · version {{VERSION}}

What Relay is

Relay is a desktop window around WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com). Everything you do in it — messaging, voice and video calls, media, your account and your data — is WhatsApp’s own software and service, loaded directly from WhatsApp’s servers. Relay only adds the window, tray icon, notifications, theme, layout and call-quality tweaks around it.

What Relay changes, on this computer only

To behave like a desktop app, Relay changes how WhatsApp Web looks and acts here: colours and layout, hiding browser-only prompts, switching on the calling feature in the page, and improving your own camera and microphone before WhatsApp receives them. Nothing is changed on WhatsApp’s servers, and nothing is sent to anyone other than WhatsApp.

Privacy

Relay has no servers, accounts or analytics. It does not read, copy or store your messages. Your login session and settings stay on this PC (%APPDATA%\Relay). Network traffic goes to WhatsApp and, for update checks, to GitHub. Known analytics and crash-report addresses used by the WhatsApp page are blocked unless you turn that off in the tray menu.
